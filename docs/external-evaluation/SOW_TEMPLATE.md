# Recorded-state evaluation agreement / statement of work

This is an operational template, not legal advice. Have appropriate counsel
review it before signature when confidentiality, intellectual property,
liability, security, or publication terms are material.

## 2. Objective

The parties will evaluate whether one identified recorded robotics or workcell
source can be converted into a bounded Metriplane fixture and evaluated under one
agreed process condition.

The possible completion classifications are `SUPPORTED`,
`PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`, and `NOT SUPPORTED`. Payment, if any, is for the agreed
work and is not conditional on a positive technical result, favorable feedback,
public praise, or endorsement.

## 4. Evaluation provider responsibilities

The provider will:

1. Review the source rights, fields, clock, coordinates, identities, and
   completeness assumptions.
2. Build or assess a bounded adapter under External Source Contract v1.
3. Preserve field-level provenance and named information loss.
4. Produce a portable fixture when the source is supportable.
5. Validate and run the fixture with the recorded Metriplane version.
6. Verify any evidence bundle and run any generated regression.
7. Deliver reproduction instructions, a technical result, and limitations.
8. Report `NOT SUPPORTED` without concealing the blocking reason when the gates
   cannot be met.

## 5. Evaluation organization responsibilities

The organization will:

1. Provide the agreed source or sample and accurate field documentation.
2. Confirm its right to provide and authorize use of the material.
3. Identify any confidentiality, security, retention, or deletion requirements.
4. Nominate a reviewer who can inspect the mapping and final result.
5. Review questions and assumptions within the agreed response time.
6. Approve any public attribution or publication separately in writing.

## 6. Deliverables

Expected deliverables, subject to the outcome classification:

- Source-fit decision.
- Adapter source or adapter limitation record.
- Source and provenance manifest.
- Entity mapping and normalization report.
- Portable normalized session and domain pack.
- Validation and deterministic-run results.
- Human-readable technical report.
- Evidence bundle verification result, when an incident exists.
- Generated regression result, when an incident exists.
- Reproduction instructions.
- Final factual acknowledgement form.

## 7. Schedule

Suggested schedule:

- Days 1 to 3: fit check and scope freeze.
- Days 4 to 10: adapter, fixture, provenance, and validation.
- Days 11 to 15: evaluation, evidence, regression, and draft report.
- Days 16 to 20: reviewer inspection, corrections, and closure.

The dates may change when source access, field semantics, or reviewer response is
delayed. A delay does not expand the technical scope automatically.

## 10. Intellectual property and licensing

- Ownership of the original source.
- Ownership of the adapter.
- License for any published adapter.
- License for any normalized fixture.
- Rights to the report and generated artifacts.
- Restrictions on redistributing source material.

Open-source publication is optional and requires explicit agreement. A public
adapter does not require publication of the original source.

## 11. Publication, attribution, and non-endorsement

No organization name, logo, quotation, raw source, fixture, or technical result
may be published unless the relevant permission option is signed.

A factual acknowledgement confirms what was reviewed or rerun. It does not state
that the organization endorses Metriplane, recommends it, or considers it ready
for production, safety, or quality use.

## 12. Technical boundaries

The evaluation is recorded, local, observe-only, and bounded. It does not include:

- Robot or machinery control.
- Safety or quality certification.
- Physical measurement validation.
- Arbitrary video understanding.
- Unrestricted anomaly detection.
- General compatibility with a source family.
- Production deployment or service-level commitments.

## 13. Stop conditions

Either party may pause or end the evaluation when:

- Rights or confidentiality cannot be established.
- Required source semantics remain unavailable.
- The source would require unknown state to be treated as absence.
- The work requires control, safety, or production authority.
- The scope changes materially.
- A security or legal concern is identified.

Completed work and agreed fees remain payable unless the signed commercial terms
state otherwise.
