## Known limitations and deferred work

- Metriplane accepts recorded, already-estimated state and supplied process
  rules. It does not establish that the source, simulator, sensor, coordinates,
  or zone labels are physically correct.
- The named repository fixtures do not establish generic compatibility with
  ManiSkill, robomimic, ROS 2, MCAP, MassRobotics, or arbitrary robotics data.
- Metriplane remains recorded, local, bounded, planar, and observe-only. It does
  not control a robot or machine, certify safety, or approve quality release.
- MP2-007, MP2-014, MP2-015, MP2-016, and MP2-017 are deferred to v0.4.1
  Assurance Hardening. v0.4.0.post1 does not pass, complete, or waive them and does
  not complete the v1.0 assurance programme.
- Historical v2.5.x authority packets are not used or recreated for this
  release.
