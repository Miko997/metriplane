# v0.4.1 assurance scope

This document records the owner decision for the unpublished v0.4.1 release.
The project has one maintainer and a zero-dollar external-infrastructure budget.
The decision changes the live proof required for this release; it does not remove
or bypass any implemented release-control behavior, engineering check, protected
main rule, or synthetic/integration test.

## Mandatory v0.4.1 assurance

The release candidate must retain its exact commit, tree, version, build inputs,
artifact bytes, checksums, and qualification identities. Protected-main health,
the governed Linux/macOS matrix, deterministic generation, traceability,
characterization, work-order and release-membership enforcement, semantic-change
governance, canonical package builds, clean wheel and source-distribution installs,
dependency and CLI checks, product/regression checks, and the final MET-162
comparison remain mandatory. Publication target occupancy must be checked before
publication. Exact-byte target readback and reconciliation remain mandatory after
publication because those observations cannot exist beforehand.

All approval, two-store retention, CAS/index/lock/LKG, promotion, reconciliation,
invalidation, interruption, recovery, stale-writer, and conflict-rejection
interfaces and tests remain part of v0.4.1. Test identities and disposable stores
prove the implemented behavior only; they do not establish live organizational or
infrastructure independence.

## Owner-deferred live assurance

The following live demonstrations are outside the v0.4.1 acceptance boundary:

- a release signature by a human who is independent of the author and maintainer;
- two independently administered external durable production evidence stores;
- a production external CAS/index/lock/LKG service; and
- multi-party privileged production deployment and authenticated readback.

They are not passed, satisfied, or simulated by the maintainer. They remain
future production-assurance requirements. MP2-207 and MP2-209 own the v0.9
independent packet, custody, restore, retention, and external-state work;
MP2-210 owns the v0.9 independent governed-release demonstration. MP2-223 and
MP2-225 retain the stronger v1.0 non-maintainer and governed-release obligations.

## Claim boundary

`SOFTWARE_ASSURANCE` may be reported `PASS` only after all mandatory v0.4.1
implementation, qualification, artifact-integrity, package, traceability,
compatibility, and installed-product gates pass on the exact final candidate.

`LIVE_MULTI_PARTY_PRODUCTION_CUSTODY` is
`DEFERRED_BY_OWNER_TO_LATER_ASSURANCE_MILESTONE`. It must never be reported as
`PASS` for v0.4.1.

Metriplane v0.4.1 implements and deterministically tests release-assurance
machinery. It does not claim independently administered multi-provider production
custody, organizationally independent release signing, production external
CAS/LKG operation, multi-party deployment authority, regulatory compliance,
production safety certification, or external adoption.
