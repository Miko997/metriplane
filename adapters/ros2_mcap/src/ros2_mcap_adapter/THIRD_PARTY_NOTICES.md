## `tf2_msgs/msg/TFMessage`

`tf2_msgs/msg/TFMessage` interface definition, expanded with the separately
licensed `geometry_msgs`, `std_msgs`, and `builtin_interfaces` dependencies.
The `tf2_msgs` source is pinned to ROS 2 `geometry2` commit
`f6053126926a38ffad5e81588054d793d87fc662`:
