Glossary

Plain labels and technical names

Recorded SessionTechnical: JSONL

A saved stream of frames that can be replayed.

Live StreamTechnical: WebSocket

The live frame connection used by browser views and clients.

Workspace CalibrationTechnical: homography

The mapping from camera pixels to real-world floor coordinates.

Printed Tracking TagsTechnical: ArUco

Markers Metriplane uses to identify important items.

Run SettingsTechnical: config

The selected cameras, profiles, zones, and runtime options.

Evidence ListTechnical: manifest

The local list of generated proof files and checksums.

Limits
What Metriplane does not claim

        
Metriplane is observe-only. It does not control machines, certify safety, approve quality release, recognize people, or claim marker-free tracking. Integrations are available when their external tools are installed.
